About EduTrack

EduTrack ERP is a web-based College Management System developed to simplify academic administration. The system provides dedicated dashboards for Students, Teachers, Parents and Administrators. Students can register through the portal, while Teacher and Parent accounts are securely created and managed by the Administrator. The platform enables efficient management of attendance, marks, notices and academic records.
